Crystal
Burst.

A scroll-driven 3D hero for the Prollyatyp0 site. The asterisk mark becomes a solid crystal that floats over the headline, cracks as you scroll, bursts into shards and drifts apart to reveal the type, then rebuilds itself.

02 · Creative concept

"Break the ordinary."

The mark is presented as something precious and finished, then it breaks open, which is what Prollyatyp0 does to ordinary products. Every shard is still part of the mark, and at the end they find their way back, so the story is deconstruct → explore → rebuild, matching the studio's design process.

The motion is restrained and physical: no glow, no particle dust, no random flashes. The drama comes from refraction (the headline bending through the glass), one sharp crack, and weighty, slowing shards.

03 · Repository research

What the references taught us

Scroll-scrubbed 3D hero (iamtanzil · "Auren Header")

A 500vh section with a sticky 100vh viewport and GSAP ScrollTrigger mapping scroll progress to the animation. We use the same structure at 400vh so the crystal is scrubbed by scroll and reverses cleanly on scroll-up.

Glass with restraint (twoclipping · "Nightshift")

Glass panels with a rim light and a strict ban on glows, particles and bouncy springs. Every move uses one shared ease. We adopt that discipline: one ease family, rim light only.

Molten-glass colour (brainextends · "Glass Tides")

Pearlescent cyan, lavender and blush in the highlights. We use a faint thin-film iridescence on the crystal edges, the same subtle colour break seen in your reference.

Light through glass (AGIOyaZ · "White Space")

Light and colour carried by the glass itself rather than added effects. The only colour in our scene comes from refraction and iridescence.

No repository had a crystal-shatter example, so the fracture is built from scratch: a Voronoi fracture of the real logo geometry, made in Blender.

04 · Visual direction

Palette & typography

Paper#E8E6E1 page
Ink#0B0B0B headline
Crystal#F4F7FB highlights
Iris cyan#B9C7FF film edge
Iris blush#FFD9C2 film edge
Stone#6F6C66 captions
Headline
Heavy grotesk, 800 weight, tight tracking (-4%), bleeding off the viewport like your current hero.
UI / captions
12 px uppercase, +14% tracking (nav, "Scroll to explore").
Lighting
Soft studio environment for reflections, one key light top-right, warm and cool rim lights behind.
Camera
50 mm, locked off, straight on. A slow 3% push-in during the burst only.
Material
Clear crystal, IOR 1.52, chamfered facets, faint frost and thin-film iridescence.
Motion
One ease family (expo-out for bursts, sine for drift). No springs, no bounce.
Sound
Only when "Sound On": glass tick on crack, one bright shatter + low thump on burst, airy tone on reform.
05 · Scene by scene

Storyboard

Timings are scroll progress through the 400vh section. Times in brackets are at a typical scroll speed. Frames are real Blender renders of the actual model.

Scene 1 · 0–15% (0–1.5 s)

Hero: the crystal

Composition
Crystal asterisk centred, headline behind it bending through the glass.
Camera
Straight on, static.
Object
Idle float: ±4° wobble and a 6 px bob on a 6 s loop, plays even before scrolling.
Technique
Three.js physical glass (transmission, thickness, iridescence).
Sound
Silent / soft room tone.
Transition
Scroll takes over from the idle loop.
Scene 2 · 15–35% (1.5–3.5 s)

The turn

Object
Scroll rotates the crystal about 40° and tilts it toward you. Light runs across the chamfered edges.
Camera
Static.
Lighting
Rim lights flare on the edges as they turn into them.
Sound
Low airy swell rising with scroll.
Transition
Rotation slows to almost a stop: the tension beat.
Scene 3 · 35–45% (3.5–4.5 s)

Fracture

Object
Thin bright crack lines race along the 20 fracture seams (≈150 ms), then the solid crystal is swapped for the 20 shards, which separate by a hair.
Camera
Static; 2 px micro-shake on the crack.
Technique
Voronoi fracture made in Blender, exported as GLB; crack lines drawn as thin emissive edges.
Sound
Sharp glass tick + creak.
Scene 4 · 45–65% (4.5–6.5 s)

Burst

Object
Shards blast outward from the centre and toward the camera, each with its own spin and speed. Fast start, long slow-down (expo-out).
Camera
3% push-in during the burst.
Effects
Headline refracts through every flying shard; no particles, no glow.
Sound
One bright shatter + low sub thump.
Scene 5 · 65–85% (6.5–8.5 s)

Drift & reveal

Object
Shards slow almost to a float and settle at the edges of the frame. The headline is fully readable.
Interaction
Shards drift slightly with the mouse (parallax by depth).
Typography
Supporting line fades up: "Design & technology studio — India".
Sound
Faint glassy chimes as shards pass.
Scene 6 · 85–100% (8.5–10 s)

Rebuild (final frame)

Object
Shards fly back and click into place; the whole crystal returns, facing the camera square-on, and the page continues to the next section.
Camera
Eases back to the start framing.
Sound
Reverse-shimmer, a soft "lock" tick.
Scroll up
The whole sequence plays backwards exactly.
