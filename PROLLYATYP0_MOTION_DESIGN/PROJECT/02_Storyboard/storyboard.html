Executive concept

The mark is built, does the work, and is whole again.

Prollyatyp0 builds software, spaces and interfaces. The film shows that as one continuous act of making. We open on the logo as a technical drawing and assemble it from lit facets. Then it opens into a product interface, an immersive world and a design canvas. In the last two seconds, one object from each world travels back and docks into the star.

Each scene looks and moves differently: a dark construction studio, a bright product shoot, a first-person VR journey, a flat design canvas and a final convergence. What stays constant is the light (soft key to one side, rim behind), the type, the brand colours, and one carried object at every handoff.

Sound design

Quiet, precise, on the beat

An original score at 120 BPM, synthesised and rendered offline like the brand film's, so no third-party audio is needed. One soft whoosh per real scene change; clicks only on real on-screen actions. The film must still read with the sound off.

SceneTimeSound
S1 · Opening0–2 sLow, soft impact at 0.0; a fine metallic tick as each facet locks on the beat; a short tonal tick on the flip.
S2 · SaaS Design & Development2–6 sA glass-like tonal tick as the app lands at 2.0; restrained tactile clicks as UI regions settle; soft low-frequency movement under the slide.
S3 · AR/VR Solutions6–10 sWide air pad; spatial pings panned to each panel; a soft low swell as the world shrinks; a haptic click on the pinch at 9.6 s.
S4 · UI/UX Design10–13 sDry, close clicks on every beat (6 states); a toggle snap; a soft keyboard patter under the typed search; a confirmation tone on the toast.
S5 · Closing13–15 sOne soft whoosh as the objects converge; the brand film's three-note motif on 13.0 / 13.5 / 14.0; a resolved tone that decays by 15.0.
